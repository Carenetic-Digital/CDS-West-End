## Navigation Structure

**Primary nav:** About Us | Services (mega-menu) | New Patients | Existing Patients | Contact Us | **Book Appointment** (CTA)

Services mega-menu lists only the categories above. Sub-pages are listed under each category.

**Footer columns:** Services | Quick Links | Resources | Contact
